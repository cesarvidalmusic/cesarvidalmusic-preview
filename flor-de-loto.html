Flor de Loto

Flor de Loto is a duo album by César Vidal and Chilean pianist Joaquín Fuentes, recorded during a period in which Vidal was beginning to shape his own voice through composition, improvisation, and the intimacy of small formats.

The album was created in Chile during a politically significant and unstable moment, marked by curfews and social tension. Despite those conditions, the two musicians were able to meet, record, and complete the project, turning the process itself into part of the album’s memory.

The music comes from a close friendship and a shared curiosity for melody, silence, and spontaneous interaction. Written ideas and improvisation appear naturally throughout the album, creating a sound that feels honest, fragile, and direct.

For Vidal, Flor de Loto documents an early and meaningful moment in his musical path: a quieter acoustic space where the saxophone and piano meet through trust, simplicity, and listening.
