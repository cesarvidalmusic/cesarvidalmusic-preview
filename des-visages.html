Des Visages

Des Visages is a Europe-based duo of saxophone and live electronics created by Chilean musicians Jorge Care and César Vidal. Their music unfolds through spontaneous interaction, weaving sonic narratives born from improvisation and blurring the boundaries between acoustic and electronic sound.

Rooted in dialogue, listening and real-time transformation, the duo builds evolving soundscapes where texture, rhythm and space shape the story as it happens. Their setup includes prepared saxophone, synthesizers, controllers, Push 3 and digital environments such as Ableton Live, MAX, TouchOSC, Beam and TouchDesigner.

Alongside sound, Des Visages incorporates a strong theatrical dimension through concert lighting, sometimes reactive to sound, as well as video projection, generated visuals, poetic texts and video mapping. Their work is also informed by electroacoustic music and by the dynamic interplay of Peter Evans and Sam Pluta, whose duo practice resonates with their own approach.

The duo released its self-titled EP in 2025, recorded and launched in Geneva, Switzerland, followed by aleas, recorded in Amsterdam and presented through their 2026 European tour, with concerts in Switzerland, France, the Netherlands, England and Denmark.

Each performance becomes a unique encounter between performer, audience and place: a sonic journey where improvisation turns sound into story.
