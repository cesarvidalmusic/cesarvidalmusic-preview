César Vidal & Artikulation Band

live at Getxo Jazz 2025

01 Sad People 02 In Memoriam of a Star

César Vidal & Artikulation Band is a multicultural contemporary jazz quintet based in the Netherlands, bringing together musicians from Chile, Italy, Portugal, and the Basque Country. The group, consisting of alto saxophone, piano, double bass, drums and electronics, explores the intersection between acoustic and electronic sound worlds through composition and improvisation, aiming to develop new sonic landscapes that challenge the boundaries of contemporary jazz.

Their repertoire spans from simple, repetitive melodic patterns to intricate structures inspired by the compositional approaches of Olivier Messiaen and Iannis Xenakis, as well as by the use of virtual composition assistants such as OpenMusic. This broad spectrum reflects the band’s interest in exploring how algorithmic thinking and formal structures can coexist with the spontaneity and expressiveness of improvisation.

Originally conceived as a project influenced by artists who have long investigated the dialogue between modern jazz ensembles and electronic media, including Steve Lehman’s Ex Machina and Demian as Posthuman, ØKSE, Peter Evans’ Ghosts and Destination : Void, Cory Smythe’s AUTOTROPHS, and Benoît Delbecq’s Gentle Ghosts, Artikulation Band finds its own language and narrative, where structure and freedom constantly inform one another.

César Vidal & Artikulation Band have performed across different regions, with notable appearances at JazzBer Festival in Bermeo and Getxo Jazz Festival in the Basque Country. At Getxo, they opened for the acclaimed vocalist Cécile McLorin Salvant and were awarded second place in the festival’s ensemble competition.
