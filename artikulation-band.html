César Vidal & Artikulation Band

César Vidal & Artikulation Band is a multicultural contemporary jazz quintet based in the Netherlands, bringing together musicians from Chile, Italy, Portugal and the Basque Country. The ensemble writes for alto saxophone, piano, double bass, drums and electronics, developing each piece through composition and collective improvisation.

Their repertoire ranges from simple, recurring melodic patterns to intricate structures inspired by contemporary classical music and virtual composition tools such as OpenMusic. Algorithmic ideas help shape the writing, while improvisation lets the musicians transform that material in performance.

Initially inspired by artists working with modern jazz ensembles and electronic media, Artikulation Band has developed its own language through this approach, giving each musician an active role in the music’s direction. Electronic sound is part of the musical material, shaping how the instruments interact and how the music unfolds.

The band has performed at JazzBer Festival in Bermeo and Getxo Jazz Festival in the Basque Country. At Getxo, they opened for three-time Grammy winner Cécile McLorin Salvant and were awarded second place in the festival’s band competition.
