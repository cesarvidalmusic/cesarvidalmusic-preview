aleas

aleas is the second recording project by Des Visages, recorded at Dijktheater in Amsterdam on March 8, 2026, and mixed and mastered by Nicolás Vera. The album emerged from the duo’s interest in improvisation in its most direct form, where musical decisions unfold entirely in real time.

Its title comes from the Latin alea, meaning “dice” or “chance”, reflecting the project’s focus on unpredictability. The album features three improvisations, alea II, alea III, and alea V, shaped through listening, risk, and immediate interaction between saxophone and live electronics.

The recording was accompanied by three video works, one for each piece, extending the duo’s exploration of lighting and real-time video projection. In this project, lighting was programmed to behave randomly in speed, intensity, and color, contrasting with earlier works where it responded directly to sound.

In 2026, Des Visages presented music developed from the same setup used to record aleas during their European tour, with concerts in Bern, Geneva, Paris, Marseille, Amsterdam, London, and Copenhagen.
